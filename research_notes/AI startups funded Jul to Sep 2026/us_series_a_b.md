# US-based AI companies: Series A / Series B rounds announced 1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026

Method: I took candidates from the Crunchbase News weekly "10 Biggest Funding Rounds" lists (11 weekly lists cover Jul 2 to Sep 18), the TechStartups daily VC roundups (about 20 days from July to September), TechCrunch, and press releases and wire stories. I then checked each company's founders, round, date and lead investors with at least one dedicated source. Design hiring was checked on 1 Oct 2026 against the public Ashby, Greenhouse, Lever and Rippling APIs, plus a careers-page scrape. A board was counted only after I confirmed that its job descriptions name the company, because several generic slugs ("encore", "prosper", "atomic", "axle", "empirical", "runetech") turned out to belong to unrelated companies and were thrown out. In the Design column, "no" means that company's own board was confirmed and had no Product, UX, UI, visual or brand design posting.

Legend: [U] = a field I could not confirm in a 2026 source (from prior knowledge or inferred). Treat it as unverified. Dates are the press-release or first-report date. Where only a roundup date was available, the date is marked "~".

## Which AI companies announced Series A or B rounds in Jul, Aug and Sep 2026?

### Takeaway
I verified 47 US-based (or US-HQ'd) AI Series A/B rounds from the quarter. The main table has 50 rows: 47 verified plus 3 flagged (River AI, Viabot, QuantHealth). Series B megarounds were led by AI creative and consumer tools: Higgsfield $400M, Meshy ~$400M, Gimlet Labs $300M, Wispr Flow $280M and Instinct $250M. Series A was dominated by AI security and agent infrastructure, for example Glow $180M, Emerald AI $150M, Prime Intellect $130M, TerraFirma $100M A, Neo $75M A and Go.AI $85M. Several rounds have ambiguous stage or HQ and are flagged below.
