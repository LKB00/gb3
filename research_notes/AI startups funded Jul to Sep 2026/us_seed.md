# US-based AI startups announcing pre-seed / seed rounds, 1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026

Method note: the main sources were the TechCrunch WordPress API (all posts matching "seed", 1 Jul–30 Sep 2026, read in full), AlleyWatch daily NYC funding reports (scraped for every business day; about 20 days 404'd or used a different URL), and targeted web searches. Hiring checks hit the public Ashby, Greenhouse and Lever APIs, guessing each company's slug (for example `api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/{slug}`). Blocked or failed sources: businessinsider.com (refused by the search tool's crawler), scouts.yutori.com (HTTP 403). Workatastartup.com was not checked. AlleyWatch only covers NYC startups, so "NYC" for AlleyWatch-sourced companies is an inference from that coverage.

## Which AI startups announced seed rounds of roughly $2M+ in Jul–Sep 2026?

### Takeaway
I verified 34 US-based (or US-headquartered) AI startups that announced pre-seed/seed rounds in the window. Sizes run from $3M to a $300M outlier. A $1.1B seed/Series A hybrid (River AI) is listed separately as row 35. The cluster is heavy on agents/enterprise AI (Harmony, June, Arga, Ando, Empirik, Hang Ten), AI infra (Keenable, Liquid Compute), physical AI (Enigma, Destro, Walden) and consumer AI (Monogram, Overtone, Ditto, Savi, Fambot). Rounds keep getting bigger: there were several $20M+ "seeds" (Fish Audio $52M, Enigma $71M, Harmony $34M, Keenable $26M, Hang Ten $85M total).
