# AI startups in Canada, Latin America, Middle East and Africa that announced funding rounds, 1 Jul to 30 Sep 2026

Method: I pulled the RSS feeds for BetaKit, LatamList, TechCabal, Disrupt Africa and Wamda to get dated funding headlines. I then fetched each article with curl and read its publication date from the `datePublished` metadata. I added Arab News "Startup Wrap" columns dated 22 Aug and 12 Sep 2026, plus aggregator pages from Wamda, Bloomberg Línea, african-startups.com and entarabi.com. For hiring, I probed the Ashby, Greenhouse, Lever and Workable APIs using slug guesses, then fetched each company's homepage and careers page and looked for design-role titles.

Blocked or limited sources: disruptafrica.com article pages returned a BlogVault firewall block to curl, so I used the RSS feed titles and other articles. The Disrupt Africa Jem article was blocked. Wamda's RSS only reached back to mid-September. MAGNiTT, Contxto, Crunchbase News and LAVCA were not checked because of time and tool budget. Most careers pages are static HTML. Where a page is JS-rendered, a "no design roles" result means no design title appeared in the fetched HTML; the true state may differ.

## Which AI companies in these regions raised in Jul–Sep 2026?

### Takeaway
I found 41 rounds from AI-first or AI-centric companies announced between 1 Jul and 30 Sep 2026: 13 in Canada, 10 in LatAm, 6 in Africa and 12 in MENA. Most are pre-seed or seed. The largest are Decade (Brazil, $85M seed), Altis Labs (Toronto, $25M Series A), Axya (Montréal, CAD 17M Series A), Synapse Analytics (Egypt, $13M Series A), Ultimarii (Calgary, CAD 13M+ Series A), Primero (Mexico City, $12M seed) and Magnar (Chile, $8M Series A). No Cohere-scale mega-round from a company headquartered in these regions turned up in the sources checked.

### Inferences
- Canada (BetaKit) gave the densest, best-documented list of AI rounds in the window. MENA and Africa AI rounds are mostly sub-$3M pre-seed or seed, with Synapse Analytics ($13M) and Whiteshield ($15M) the exceptions.
- I found no frontier-lab mega-round from these regions in Q3 2026; the largest regional AI round was Decade's $85M seed.

### Gaps
- Exact announcement dates are unverified for Inner AI, Cue, HyperDev, Kuadra (July), RIME (August), Whiteshield, AVELIN AI, DESAISIV and MOZN.
