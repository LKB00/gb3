## Which Indian and APAC AI companies raised in Jul–Sep 2026?

### Takeaway
I found 38 in-window AI deals: 24 India-linked and 14 rest of APAC. There is also a short borderline list of 3. India is dominated by application-layer AI: coding agents, voice AI, sales/GTM AI and enterprise agents. The largest Indian rounds were Emergent ($130M Series C, unicorn), Ema ($77M), Freehand ($75M), Sarvam ($74M Series B extension) and Flam ($40M). The largest APAC rounds were PixVerse (China, $439M Series C total), Heidi (Australia, US$340M), Acrab (Singapore, $130M), PsiBot (China, >$100M) and Wrtn (Korea, ~₩100B/$72M).
