# US AI companies: Series C+, growth and $100M+ mega-rounds announced 1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026

Method and caveats (for the report writer):
- The main deal source is the Crunchbase News weekly "Week's 10 Biggest Funding Rounds" columns for the weeks ending Jul 2, Jul 10, Jul 17, Jul 24, Jul 31, Aug 7, Aug 14, Aug 21, Aug 28, Sep 4, Sep 11, Sep 18 and Sep 25, 2026. Each was read in full through WebFetch. Company press releases and trade press filled in or checked specific deals.
- **Dates:** where only the Crunchbase week is known, the date is written as "wk ending <date>" (the round was announced in the 7 days up to that date). An exact date is given only where a source showed it.
- **Founders:** I did not verify founders against a source in this pass (tool budget). Founder names are tagged **[unverified, background knowledge]**, and "?" means unknown. The report writer should verify them or mark them unverified.
- **Website URLs** are the company's known primary domain and were not fetched, except where an ATS board confirmed the company name. Treat them as unverified.
- **Design hiring** was checked on 1 Oct 2026 through the public ATS APIs (Ashby, Greenhouse, Lever), filtered for "design/UX" titles. Chip, RTL, civil and data-center "design" roles were excluded. Board ownership was confirmed by the Ashby page title or the Greenhouse board name for Temporal, Glow, Owner.com, Factory, Numeral, Simile, Profound, Rillet, Atoms and Island. "not checked" means I found no public ATS board under the slugs I tried. It does **not** mean the company has no design roles.
- **Blocked sites:** news.crunchbase.com returns a Cloudflare block to curl, but WebFetch worked. I did not access Bloomberg, The Information or Axios Pro Rata (paywalled).
- **Exclusions:** 8090 (its $135M was a **Series A**, announced 26 Jun, [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260626795833/en/8090-Raises-$135M-Series-A-to-Accelerate-Their-Rollout-of-Software-Factory)). Seed, Series A and Series B rounds in the window, including Meshy, Instinct, Higgsfield, Wispr Flow, Arcee, Generalist AI, Gimlet Labs, Upwind, HiddenLayer, Volta, River AI, Lumilens, Velaura and Prime Intellect. Non-US companies: Mistral (€3B Series D, 8 Sep), Moonshot, Kling and Emergent (an Indian AI coding startup; $130M Series C at a $1.5B valuation led by Creaegis, [Unite.ai](https://www.unite.ai/emergent-raises-130-million-series-c-at-1-5-billion-valuation/), so it can be added if the report treats it as US-linked). Clearly non-AI companies: The Boring Co., Stoke Space, Castelion, Hadrian, Base Power, Whatnot and others.

## Which AI companies raised Series C+ / growth / mega rounds in Jul–Sep 2026?

### Takeaway
I found 45 US-based AI or AI-infrastructure companies with qualifying rounds announced between 1 Jul and 30 Sep 2026. Most of the money went to AI compute and infrastructure: Crusoe ($3B), Fluidstack ($1.5B), Fireworks ($1.5B), SambaNova ($1B), Etched ($300M plus $700M), Together ($800M) and Positron ($500M). The other large rounds went to frontier labs (SSI, $5B), data and AI platforms (Databricks, $5B at $190B), AI coding (Cognition at $2B/$48B, Factory, CodeRabbit) and vertical AI (Harvey, EliseAI, Norm AI, Rillet, Numeral, Owner.com).
