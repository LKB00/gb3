## Which European, UK and Israeli AI companies raised in Jul–Sep 2026?

### Takeaway
I found 60+ qualifying AI rounds in the window. The biggest are Mistral (EUR3B Series D, 8 Sep), Helsing ($1.8B Series E, Jul), Wonderful ($550M Series C, 1–2 Sep), CuspAI ($450M Series B, Jul), Lovable ($400M Series C, 12 Aug), OLIX ($312M Series B, ~3 Aug) and Callosum ($100M seed, Aug). Many of the named "types" did NOT raise in this window: ElevenLabs only ran an employee tender, and Legora, Parloa, Synthesia, Wayve and Black Forest Labs all raised outside it. Israeli rounds in the period were dominated by AI-agent security.
