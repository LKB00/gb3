# AI-native companies with US HQ and an India team that raised money Jan 2025 – Sep 2026

Research date: 2026-09-30. Budget-limited pass (about 20 tool calls). Every amount, date and investor below comes from the linked source. Where HQ or the India office was not confirmed, the entry says so.

## Which US-HQ AI companies with India presence raised (seed to late stage) in 2025–2026?

### Takeaway
At least 11 AI-first companies with US HQ or incorporation and a Bengaluru-heavy team raised money in the window. They cover agent infra and dev tools (Composio, Portkey, TrueFoundry, Lyzr, UnifyApps), vibe-coding apps (Emergent), voice and support AI (Giga, Smallest.ai), IT/service agents (Atomicwork), compliance AI (AiPrise) and AI marketing (Gushwork). Several raised twice in the window (Emergent, Lyzr, Smallest.ai, Composio).

### Cited Findings

**Atomicwork**
- Product: agentic service management for enterprise IT (ITSM). The platform combines an enterprise knowledge graph with AI agents.
- Round: $25M Series A, January 2025. Led by Khosla Ventures and Z47. Battery Ventures, Blume Ventures and Peak XV also took part. — [FinSMEs](https://www.finsmes.com/2025/01/atomicwork-raises-25m-in-series-a-funding.html); [Atomicwork blog](https://www.atomicwork.com/blog/atomicwork-series-a-funding)
- India office: Bengaluru. Its Greenhouse job listings are all in Bengaluru, Karnataka. — [Greenhouse board](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/atomicwork)
- US HQ city: not confirmed in the sources I fetched (see Gaps).

**Composio**
- Product: infrastructure that lets AI agents use apps such as Gmail, Salesforce, GitHub and Slack, plus "skills" that improve over time.
- Round: $25M Series A led by Lightspeed Venture Partners. Elevation Capital and Together Fund participated, along with angels including Gokul Rajaram, Dharmesh Shah and Guillermo Rauch. Total raised is $29M including a $4M seed.
- Offices: San Francisco and Bengaluru. — [Entrepreneur India](https://india.entrepreneur.com/news-and-trends/composio-raises-usd-25-mn-from-lightspeed-to-advance/494938); [SiliconANGLE, 22 Jul 2025](https://siliconangle.com/2025/07/22/composio-raises-25m-funding-ease-ai-agent-development/)
- Date conflict: SiliconANGLE dates the round July 2025. A later PR Newswire release says "Composio Raises $29M" (the cumulative figure), and its release ID suggests 2025–26. — [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/composio-raises-29m-to-solve-ais-learning-problem-building-skills-that-actually-improve-over-time-302510684.html)

**Emergent**
- Product: a "vibe coding" platform where AI agents build full-stack apps from plain-language prompts.
- Round 1: $23M Series A, 24 Sep 2025. Led by Lightspeed, with YC, Together Fund and Prosus participating. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/24/emergent-raises-23m-from-lightspeed-to-let-consumers-build-apps/)
- Round 2: $70M Series B, 20 Jan 2026, at a $300M post-money valuation. Led by SoftBank Vision Fund 2 and Khosla Ventures, with Prosus, Lightspeed, Together and YC participating. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/20/indian-vibe-coding-startup-emergent-raises-70m-at-300m-valuation-from-softbank-khosla-ventures/)
- HQ: San Francisco. India team: 70 of its 75 employees are in Bengaluru (as of Jan 2026). — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/20/indian-vibe-coding-startup-emergent-raises-70m-at-300m-valuation-from-softbank-khosla-ventures/)

**Giga (formerly GigaML)**
- Product: human-quality AI voice and chat agents for customer support. DoorDash is a customer.
- Round: $61M Series A, November 2025. Led by Redpoint, with Y Combinator and Nexus Venture Partners participating. Founded by IIT Kharagpur graduates. — [Fortune, 5 Nov 2025](https://fortune.com/2025/11/05/voice-ai-giga-raise-61-million-customer-service-series-a/); [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/giga-nets-61-mn-to-build-ai-voice-agents-for-customer-care-centres/)
- US HQ city and India office: not verified in the fetched sources (see Gaps).

**Portkey**
- Product: an AI gateway and "control plane" for LLM apps, covering routing, governance, observability and cost tracking.
- Round: $15M Series A, 19 Feb 2026. Led by Elevation Capital, with Lightspeed participating. — [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/02/19/3241385/0/en/Portkey-Raises-15M-Series-A-to-Scale-the-Unified-Control-Plane-for-Production-AI.html)
- HQ and India office: not verified here.

**TrueFoundry**
- Product: a platform for deploying and running AI and LLM workloads and agents.
- Round: $19M Series A, February 2025. Led by Intel Capital, with Eniac, Peak XV's Surge and Jump Capital participating. — [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250206649881/en/TrueFoundry-Secures-%2419-Million-Series-A-Funding-to-Transform-AI-Deployment-at-Scale-Powered-by-their-Agent-on-Autopilot)
- HQ and India office: not verified here.

**Lyzr**
- Product: an agent studio / "agentic OS" where enterprises, especially in banking and insurance, build AI agents.
- Round 1: $8M Series A, October 2025, led by Rocketship.vc. Accenture Ventures, GFT, Firstsource and others participated. — [Lyzr blog](https://www.lyzr.ai/blog/lyzr-raising-series-a/)
- Round 2: $14.5M Series A+, March 2026, led by Accenture at a $250M valuation. — [Bloomberg, 9 Mar 2026](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-09/agentic-ai-startup-lyzr-raises-funds-at-250-million-valuation); [citybiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/816946/accenture-leads-14-5m-series-a-for-lyzrs-ai-agents-push/)
- HQ: Jersey City, NJ, per Tracxn (an aggregator). — [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/lyzr/__3iNuwF28pWvfOkygQJziJIfs1U-wu38eTORcpSBqjqo)
- India office: not verified. It is commonly reported as Bengaluru, but I did not confirm this.

**UnifyApps**
- Product: a low-code "enterprise operating system for AI" that connects systems of record such as Salesforce and Workday to generative AI.
- Round: $50M Series B, 22 Oct 2025. Led by WestBridge Capital, with ICONIQ participating. Total raised is $81M, and Ragy Thomas joined as Chairman and Co-CEO. — [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/10/22/3171140/0/en/UnifyApps-raises-50M-to-become-the-Enterprise-Operating-System-for-AI-to-help-CIOs-succeed-with-GenAI.html); [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/news/unifyapps-raises-50-mn-in-series-b-led-by-westbridge-10584514)
- HQ and India office: not verified here.

**Smallest.ai**
- Product: real-time voice AI (speech recognition, language understanding and speech synthesis) for contact centres.
- Round 1: $8M seed, 28 Oct 2025. Led by Sierra Ventures, with 3one4 and Better Capital participating. Offices: San Francisco and Bengaluru. — [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/snippets/smallestai-raises-8-mn-in-seed-funding-led-by-sierra-ventures-10600511)
- Round 2: $13M Series A, 31 Jul 2026. Led by Seligman Ventures, with Sierra and 3one4 participating. Total raised is more than $21M. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/31/smallest-ai-raises-13m-to-build-ultra-fast-voice-ai-that-sounds-genuinely-human/)

**AiPrise**
- Product: AI-driven KYC/KYB and global compliance.
- Round: $12.5M Series A, 8 Oct 2025. — [Axios Pro](https://www.axios.com/pro/fintech-deals/2025/10/08/aiprise-12-million-global-compliance)
- Lead investor: not captured (Axios Pro is paywalled).
- India office: it has a Bengaluru "Product Designer II" opening. — [Ashby](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/aiprise/8b61b339-a456-4f33-9970-19a69caa0db4)

**Gushwork**
- Product: AI marketing agents that get small and mid-size businesses found in AI search. The agents generate content, build backlinks and track leads.
- Round: $9M seed, February 2026. Led by SIG and Lightspeed, with B Capital, Beenext and others participating. Total raised is $11M.
- HQ: Delaware. India: about 70 employees in Bengaluru. — [TechCrunch, 25 Feb 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/25/gushwork-bets-on-ai-search-for-customer-leads-and-early-results-are-emerging/)

**Rocketlane** (professional-services automation, now pitched as agentic with its "Nitro" platform)
- Round: $60M Series C, led by Insight Partners. Total raised is $105M. It has offices in New York, San Francisco and London, and was founded in Chennai. — [SiliconANGLE, 25 Mar 2026](https://siliconangle.com/2026/03/25/rocketlane-bags-60m-investors-accelerate-professional-services-automation-ai-agents/)
- Caveats: its roots are SaaS rather than AI-native. Its $24M Series B was earlier and falls outside the window. — [Rocketlane press](https://www.rocketlane.com/press/rocketlane-raises-24-million-in-series-b-funding)

**SpotDraft** (AI contract lifecycle management and on-device contract review)
- Round 1: $54M Series B, February 2025. Led by Vertex Growth Singapore and Trident Growth Partners. — [SpotDraft blog](https://www.spotdraft.com/blog/spotdraft-secures-54-million-to-lead-ai-contract-lifecycle-management)
- Round 2: $8M Series B extension from Qualcomm Ventures, 26 Jan 2026. Valuation is nearing $400M. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/26/qualcomm-backs-spotdraft-to-scale-on-device-contract-ai-with-valuation-doubling-toward-400m/)
- US HQ vs India HQ: not verified here.

### Inferences
- Bengaluru is almost always the India hub. Companies like Emergent (93% of staff in India) and Gushwork (about 70 in India) run most of their product and engineering work from India, so India-based design roles there are plausible.
- The strongest leads for design jobs are the companies that raised most recently: Smallest.ai (Jul 2026), Rocketlane (Mar 2026), Lyzr (Mar 2026), Portkey and Gushwork (Feb 2026), and Emergent and SpotDraft (Jan 2026).

### Gaps
- I did not confirm the US HQ city and India office for Atomicwork, Giga, Portkey, TrueFoundry, UnifyApps, SpotDraft or AiPrise.
- AiPrise's lead investor is behind the Axios paywall.
- I did not research these candidates because of the call budget: Ema, Uniphore, Observe.AI, Glean, Atlan, Simplismart, SigNoz, CloudSEK, Adya, Ringg, Bolna and Vahan.
- The Composio round is dated July 2025 by SiliconANGLE, but there is a separate "$29M" PR Newswire release with an unclear date.

## Do they list product/UX design roles, and how big is the India team?

### Takeaway
I confirmed open design roles in Bengaluru at Atomicwork and AiPrise. Most other job boards render with JavaScript or use applicant tracking systems I couldn't query, so for those companies it is unknown, not "no roles". India team size was reported only for Emergent and Gushwork.

### Cited Findings
- **Atomicwork:** three design roles on Greenhouse, all in Bengaluru, Karnataka: "Lead Product Designer (Bangalore, India)", "Product Designer" and "Visual Designer". The board has 27 roles in total. — [Lead PD](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/atomicwork/jobs/4170401008); [Product Designer](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/atomicwork/jobs/4407185008); [Visual Designer](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/atomicwork/jobs/4177607008)
- **AiPrise:** "Product Designer II" in Bengaluru, one of 16 roles on its Ashby board. — [Ashby](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/aiprise/8b61b339-a456-4f33-9970-19a69caa0db4)
- **Composio:** 29 roles on its Ashby board, none with "design" in the title as of 2026-09-30. — [Ashby](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/composio)
- **SpotDraft:** 15 roles on Ashby, none with "design" in the title. — [Ashby](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/spotdraft)
- **Lyzr:** its Ashby board had 0 roles listed.
- **Emergent:** 75 employees, 70 of them in Bengaluru (Jan 2026). — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/20/indian-vibe-coding-startup-emergent-raises-70m-at-300m-valuation-from-softbank-khosla-ventures/)
- **Gushwork:** about 70 employees in Bengaluru plus contractors. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/25/gushwork-bets-on-ai-search-for-customer-leads-and-early-results-are-emerging/)

### Inferences
- Atomicwork is hiring designers at several levels right now (lead, mid-level and visual), which makes it the best-matched lead for a mid-level product designer. AiPrise's "Product Designer II" is a direct mid-level match.

### Gaps
- These boards couldn't be queried: Emergent, Giga, Portkey, Rocketlane, UnifyApps, Gushwork, Smallest.ai, TrueFoundry and Rocket.new. They use a board slug I didn't find or an applicant tracking system that renders client-side. The rocketlane.com/careers page returned 404, and jobs.ashbyhq.com pages render empty through WebFetch.
- Design roles on those boards need to be checked by hand, via the careers pages or LinkedIn.
- India headcount was not reported for most of the companies.
