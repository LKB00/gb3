# Remote design roles at US-HQ AI-product companies open to India (as of 30 Sep 2026)

Method: Pulled the Himalayas search API with `country=India` (returns only jobs whose location restrictions include India or are worldwide) for "product designer", "ux designer", "ui designer", "design engineer", "ai designer", "interaction designer" (77 unique jobs). Also pulled Remote OK API (`tag=design`), Remotive API (`category=design`), We Work Remotely design RSS, Working Nomads API, Wellfound product-designer role page, Dribbble jobs, YC Work at a Startup. Confirmed on company ATS (Lever/Ashby APIs) where possible. Dates are the board's posted date.

## What AI-company design roles open to India appear on the boards?

### Takeaway
Only a handful of live postings meet all three tests (US-HQ, AI-native product, India-eligible remote). The strongest are HeyMarvin Product Designer (India-remote, INR salary, 5+ yrs), Netomi Staff Product Designer (India, 8–12 yrs), Kick Design Engineer (worldwide, 5+ yrs eng), and Fireflies.ai Product Designer (3+ yrs, but "anywhere in your respective country" wording is ambiguous). For a 3–4 year designer, Fireflies.ai and the Zenara Health roles (HQ unverified) are the closest fits; most US AI firms ask 5+ years.

### Inferences
- Pattern: US AI companies that hire designers in India do so via India-restricted remote postings with INR pay (HeyMarvin, Netomi, HighLevel), not "worldwide" USD postings.
- Truly worldwide US AI design roles are rare and skew to design engineers (Kick) or AI-training contracts (Mercor).
- For a 3–4 yr designer: best bets are Fireflies.ai (confirm India eligibility), Zenara/xponentiate, Winbold; HeyMarvin and Brandlive are stretch applications (5+ / 4–5 yrs).
