# US AI Labs and Scale-ups with India Offices/Teams, and Design Hiring There (as of 30 Sep 2026)

Method note: India/design roles were checked directly against public ATS JSON APIs on 30 Sep 2026 (Ashby `api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/{org}`, Greenhouse `boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/{org}/jobs`, Lever, Workable). Role lists are a point-in-time snapshot. Boards not found under the slugs tried (so NOT checked): Fireworks AI (Greenhouse; Ashby `fireworks` board exists with 86 jobs, 0 India), Moveworks, Gong, Eightfold, Hugging Face (Workable), Skild AI, Glean-on-Ashby. newsbytesapp.com returned 403 to WebFetch.

## Which US AI labs and scale-ups have announced or are operating India offices/teams?

### Takeaway
The two frontier labs are both in India now. Anthropic's Bengaluru office opened on 17 Feb 2026. OpenAI runs New Delhi and announced Mumbai and Bengaluru for later in 2026. Established scale-ups (Databricks, Glean, Harvey) run real Bengaluru engineering hubs. Cursor, Cognition, Together AI and Cresta are hiring in India, mostly for go-to-market and engineering. A wide sweep of ATS boards found almost no design roles in India at US AI companies. The only design-titled India roles seen were at Cresta (remote-India) and Observe.AI (Bengaluru).

### Inferences
- US AI labs are building their India teams around go-to-market first: sales, applied AI/FDE, partnerships and policy. Product and design stay concentrated in SF/NY. Anthropic, OpenAI and Cursor all fit this pattern. Design hiring in India, if it comes, probably follows once engineering hubs mature.
- Companies with established Bengaluru engineering and product hubs (Glean with India PMs, Databricks, Harvey) are the likeliest US AI firms to open product-design roles in India next. Glean's India product managers are the strongest signal of this.
- "Remote" design roles at these US companies were almost always US/North America- or Europe-restricted (for example Linear, Harvey and ElevenLabs). None of the design roles in the sweep was explicitly open to India-remote, apart from Cresta's conversational-design role.

### Gaps
- No ATS data was retrieved for Eightfold AI, Moveworks (now part of ServiceNow), Gong, Hugging Face, Fireworks AI (India), Skild AI or Uniphore. All are believed to have India teams, but I did not verify this.
- Latest funding is unverified for Together AI, Skild AI, Scale AI, Sierra, Decagon, Perplexity, Replit and Vercel (outside the tool budget).
- The Harvey Sep 2026 round and the Cursor acquisition close date come from search-summary snippets and need primary confirmation.
- The ATS snapshot covers 30 Sep 2026 only. Some companies post India roles on LinkedIn or their own careers pages rather than the public ATS feed, so a "no design roles" finding is not proof that none exist.
- It is not confirmed whether OpenAI's Bengaluru and Mumbai offices had physically opened by Sep 2026. Job postings list Mumbai and Bangalore-remote, but no opening announcement was found.
