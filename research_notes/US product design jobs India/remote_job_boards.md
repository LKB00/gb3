# Remote Product Designer openings on remote-first job boards: US-HQ companies, open to India/APAC/worldwide (as of 30 Sep 2026)

Method note: I pulled the public JSON/RSS feeds of Remotive, Remote OK, We Work Remotely (design RSS), Working Nomads (API) and Himalayas (search API, `country=IN`, 5 pages each for "product designer", "product design", "ux designer", "ui ux designer"). I then checked each shortlisted role on the company's own ATS where I could reach it. Himalayas job pages return HTTP 403 (a Cloudflare challenge) to direct fetch, so the Himalayas data comes from its API. Cited Himalayas URLs are the job `guid`/`applicationLink` values the API returned.

## Which qualifying postings exist, and their details (company, title, HQ, eligibility, experience, salary, date, apply URL, board)?

### Takeaway
I found only 3 full-time roles that meet every criterion: a US HQ confirmed, remote with India or worldwide explicitly allowed, a mid-level experience requirement (5 years or less), and a posting that is still open. They are **Brandlive – Product Designer** (India team, 4–5 yrs), **HeyMarvin – Product Designer** (fully remote across India, 5+ yrs, INR 20–28 LPA) and **Chess.com – Product Designer, Growth** (open globally, 2–3 yrs, 3-hour overlap with US hours). **Mercor** adds 2 US-HQ contract roles that list India. Most "worldwide" product-design listings on these boards come from non-US companies, or are Lead/Staff/Senior roles asking for 7+ years.

### Inferences
- The realistic shortlist for a 3–4-year designer in India is Chess.com Growth (2–3 yrs, global) and Brandlive (4–5 yrs, India team). HeyMarvin (5+) is a stretch, but its explicit INR band makes it attractive.
- US-HQ companies that open mid-level product-design roles to India usually do it in one of two ways. Some hire an India-specific team (Brandlive, HeyMarvin, HighLevel, Rippling). Others hire globally with a US-hours overlap requirement (Chess.com). Pure "worldwide" listings on these boards come mostly from non-US companies.
- Himalayas' `country=IN` filter includes listings with no location restriction ("worldwide"). The description text sometimes contradicts that tag (EWOR, micro1), so check eligibility in the posting itself.
