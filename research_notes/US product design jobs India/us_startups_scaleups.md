# US-HQ SaaS / AI startups & scale-ups: Product Designer openings in India / remote-India (as of 30 Sep 2026)

Method note: Most rows were pulled directly from the companies' public ATS APIs/boards on 30 Sep 2026 (Greenhouse board API, Lever postings API, Ashby posting API, SmartRecruiters API, Workday CXS search, Rippling ATS API, Innovaccer careers page linking to Workable). Where an ATS API lists a job, it is live on the board as of the query date. Dates: Lever `createdAt`, SmartRecruiters `releasedDate`, Workday `startDate` = posting date; Greenhouse `updated_at` / Ashby `publishedAt` are last-updated/published timestamps, not necessarily original post dates.
