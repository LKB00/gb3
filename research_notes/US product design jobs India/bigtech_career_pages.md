# US Big-Tech / Fintech / Retail-Tech Career Pages: Mid-Level Product/UX Design Openings in India (as of 30 Sep 2026)

Method: I queried the companies' own job APIs or career pages directly (Workday CXS JSON API, amazon.jobs search.json, Google Careers HTML, Microsoft apply.careers.microsoft.com PCSX API, Greenhouse boards API, SmartRecruiters API, Oracle HCM REST for Oracle and JPMC, Intuit's Radancy pages) on 30 Sep 2026, and used WebSearch where no API was reachable. "Open" means the role showed up in the company's live search results or job API on 30 Sep 2026. For Workday roles, "canApply: true" was also confirmed. Posting dates are the ones the source reported.

## Which qualifying mid-level (about 3–5 yrs) product/UX design roles are open in India?

### Takeaway
Only 3 postings were confirmed live on the company's own career site and fit the 3–5 year band: **Google "UX Designer, YouTube Creator Support" (Bengaluru, 4 yrs)**, **Amazon "UX Designer I, EU INTech Partner Growth Experience" (Bengaluru, 4+ yrs)** and **HP "Interaction and Motion designer" (Bengaluru, ~5 yrs)**. **Uber** shows three "Product Designer II" roles in Bangalore in search results, but its site blocked automated checks, so their status is unconfirmed. Most other India design openings at these companies are Senior/Staff/Principal roles asking for 6–10+ years.

### Inferences
- Openings for 3–4-year-experience designers in India at these companies are scarce. Most India design requisitions are Senior/Staff/Principal. Google and Amazon, and probably Uber, are the main exceptions.
- Amazon's "Product Designer" title in India does not mean mid-level: ID 10560185 asks for 7+ years. Titles alone are unreliable, so check the stated years.
- Target's Sr UX Designer (6+ yrs) and Uber's PD II roles (4–8 yrs per aggregator) are the closest stretch options.
