# Mid-level Product Designer openings in India at US-HQ companies (India job portals, as of 30 Sep 2026)

Method note: Research was run on 30 Sep 2026. LinkedIn's public (logged-out) job search pages for Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Gurugram/Delhi NCR and Pune loaded, and so did individual LinkedIn job pages. Naukri and Indeed India returned HTTP 403 (proxy/anti-bot block). A few LinkedIn job pages returned HTTP 429 (rate limit). Glassdoor, Instahyre, Cutshort, Hirist, Foundit and Wellfound were not opened, because the budget was spent on LinkedIn, which returned usable data. Posting dates are LinkedIn's relative stamps ("6 days ago"), converted to approximate calendar dates counting back from 30 Sep 2026. No company ATS (career-site) links were retrieved: the LinkedIn pages only show a LinkedIn "Apply" flow when logged out. The Nutanix career URL surfaced by search returned 404, so that role is probably closed.

## What postings appear on the portals for these titles and locations?

### Takeaway
LinkedIn has the most usable data. It shows about 1,000+ Product Designer jobs in Bengaluru and 334 in Pune. After filtering to US-HQ companies with a stated experience requirement of 5 years or less, five postings were confirmed live with experience ranges:
- Nielsen – Product Designer II, Bengaluru, 2–4 yrs
- Tekion – Product Designer II, Bengaluru, 3–5 yrs
- HPE – UX Product Designer (Networking), Bengaluru, hybrid, 3–5 yrs
- Envoy Global – Product Designer, Hyderabad, 2–4 yrs
- Thermo Fisher – Product Designer, AI & Enterprise Software, Bengaluru, 3–7+ yrs

Several other US-HQ postings were found, but either their experience range is unverified or they ask for 5+/6+ years.
