# Product Designer openings in India / Remote-India on Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby (US-HQ companies), as of 30 Sep 2026

Method: I queried the public ATS JSON APIs directly (boards-api.greenhouse.io, api.lever.co/v0/postings, api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board) for about 700 candidate company slugs. I filtered for design titles with Indian or APAC locations, then pulled each matching posting's full description to get its experience requirement. I also ran 3 WebSearch `site:` queries to find companies outside my slug list and checked those against the APIs too. An API listing means the posting is live on the board as of 30 Sep 2026. "Updated" is the Greenhouse `updated_at` field. "Posted" is Greenhouse `first_published`, Lever `createdAt` or Ashby `publishedAt`.

## Which roles show up on Greenhouse / Lever / Ashby for these titles with India locations?

Seven live postings fit the brief (mid-level, experience asked ≤5 years at the low end, India location, company likely US-HQ). Next are **AiPrise Product Designer II** (Bengaluru, 3+ yrs), **Ema Product Designer** (Bengaluru hybrid, 3+ yrs), **Zeta Global Product Designer** (Bengaluru, 4+ yrs), **Atomicwork Product Designer** (Bengaluru hybrid, 1–4 yrs), **Hevo Data Senior Product Designer** (Bengaluru, 4–8 yrs) and **SigNoz Sr Product Designer** (Remote India, 4–10 yrs). ### Inferences
- The best fits for a 3–4-year designer are Tekion PD II, AiPrise PD II, Ema PD and Zeta Global PD.
- Atomicwork's 1–4 yr ask is slightly junior-leaning. It was first published in Apr 2025, so it may be an evergreen or pipeline req.
- Hevo's and SigNoz's "Senior" roles have a low-end ask of 4 years, which fits the "Senior only if ≤5 years" rule.
- Most India product-design demand on these boards comes from mid-size US-incorporated startups with large Indian engineering centres (Tekion, Hevo, Atomicwork, Ema, AiPrise, SigNoz), not from big-cap US tech. Large US companies mostly post Staff/Manager-level design roles in India.
- Zeta Global (NYSE: ZETA, New York) is a different company from Zeta (Zeta Suite, Bengaluru). The `zeta` Lever board had no design roles in India.
