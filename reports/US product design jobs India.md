# Sixteen live roles fit a 3–4-year designer

As of 30 Sep 2026, the research found **16 open Product/UX Designer roles at US-headquartered (or US-incorporated) companies that fit a designer with about 3–4 years of experience and can be done from India**. The strongest are **Tekion Product Designer II** (Bengaluru, 3–5 yrs), **HPE UX Product Designer** (Bengaluru hybrid, 3–5 yrs), **Ema Product Designer** (Bengaluru hybrid, 3+ yrs) and **AiPrise Product Designer II** (Bengaluru, 3+ yrs). **Nielsen, Google, Brandlive and Zeta Global** follow close behind. Only three of the 16 are remote from India (Brandlive, Chess.com and SigNoz). The rest are office or hybrid roles in Bengaluru, Hyderabad or Noida. Mid-level openings are scarce. Most India design requisitions at big US tech (Adobe, Okta, ServiceNow, Broadcom, Rippling, Freshworks, Sprinklr) are Senior, Staff, Lead or Manager roles asking for 6–10+ years. Much of the real mid-level demand comes from US-incorporated startups with large Bengaluru teams (Tekion, Hevo, Atomicwork, Ema, AiPrise, SigNoz), which a job seeker may read as Indian companies. Three more pools are worth pursuing: 3 Uber "Product Designer II" roles in Bangalore that could not be verified, about 14 roles just above the band (5–7 yrs) worth a stretch application, and 2 Mercor contract gigs. Coverage has real holes. Naukri, Indeed India, Glassdoor, Instahyre and Wellfound were not read, and several large career sites (Uber, Walmart, Intuit, AmEx, Qualcomm, Dell, Meta, LinkedIn, Goldman Sachs) were blocked or not checked. Treat this as a strong starting list, not a census.

The top four share one trait: the posting names a band that starts at or near 3 years and is confirmed live. **Tekion is the single best bet.** Its 3–5-year band, B2B/enterprise focus and advanced-Figma ask match a 3–4-year profile closely. It was verified on two sources and appears alongside a separate Senior track (5–7 yrs), which signals a real levelled design ladder in Bangalore ([Ashby](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/tekion/2064cc74-84ca-4b16-9615-40a73e321c68)). The LinkedIn copy already shows **200+ applicants**, so apply fast and apply through the ATS ([LinkedIn](https://in.linkedin.com/jobs/view/product-designer-ii-at-tekion-corp-4463471044)). HPE is the strongest big-company option because its 3–5-year floor is stated plainly and it is only days old. Ema is the only top-ranked startup whose posting itself confirms a Silicon Valley HQ ([Ashby](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/ema/36c17dbb-168d-4654-9a4e-0be64cacd02a)).

Lower in the table, fit depends on where exactly the candidate sits in the 3–4-year range. Google's and Amazon's 4-year floors are hard minimums, so a 3-year designer should apply there only with a strong portfolio. Amazon's role was posted in May and has been open about four months ([amazon.jobs](https://www.amazon.jobs/en/jobs/10431190/ux-designer-i-eu-intech-partner-growth-experience)). Titles also mislead in both directions. Amazon's plain "Product Designer, Amazon Pay India" asks for **7+ years**, while Hevo's "Senior" and SigNoz's "Sr" roles start at **4 years** ([Hevo Lever](https://jobs.lever.co/hevodata/3c22a84f-7eb6-4105-aa3b-7698322a98af); [SigNoz Ashby](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/signoz/ea04f41f-62c2-40a2-996f-6d7389d80124)). Envoy Global and Chess.com lean junior, so a 4-year designer is at the top of their bands. Chess.com is, however, one of only three roles here that genuinely lets someone work from home in India, and it requires just a 3-hour overlap with US hours ([Rippling](https://ats.rippling.com/chess/jobs/578f7e07-3ca1-4d3e-a670-2d5e5ec8bcf8)). Brandlive's posting, by contrast, explicitly recruits for its "India product team" ([Himalayas](https://himalayas.app/companies/brandlive/jobs/product-designer)).

## Conclusion

For a 3–4-year designer, being able to work from India is no longer the limiting factor; what is scarce is openings at their level. India-based design teams at US companies clearly exist, but they are being built top-down. The current wave of Lead, Staff and Manager requisitions at Rippling, Freshworks, Sprinklr, Okta and ServiceNow implies mid-level backfills in the coming quarters, which makes the watch list as actionable as the master table. The practical play is to apply now to the Tekion, HPE, Ema, AiPrise, Nielsen and Google roles. Anyone at 4 years should also send stretch applications to the roles that start at exactly 5 years (HeyMarvin, AmEx, HP, Seismic, StarRez). Then check Uber, Innovaccer and Whatfix by hand, since their sites could not be read.

A second takeaway is about process. Many of the confirmed roles were found only by querying ATS boards directly. Portal listings were often stale (Intuit, Sprinklr, BrowserStack, Microsoft), and the big Indian portals could not be read at all. A job seeker who monitors the Ashby, Greenhouse and Lever boards of US-incorporated Bengaluru startups will see openings earlier than one relying on Naukri or LinkedIn. Earlier matters when a mid-level LinkedIn posting like Tekion's already has 200+ applicants.
