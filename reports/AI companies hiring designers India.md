# Bengaluru startups, not AI labs, hire Indian designers

As of 30 Sep 2026, a designer in India with 3–4 years of experience has **four realistic applications at US-headquartered AI-product companies**. They are **Ema's Product Designer** (Bengaluru hybrid, 3+ yrs), **AiPrise's Product Designer II** (Bengaluru onsite, 3+ yrs), **Atomicwork's Product Designer** (Bengaluru hybrid, 1–4 yrs) and **Fireflies.ai's remote Product Designer** (3+ yrs, though it is unclear whether India-based applicants qualify). Six more roles are reachable as stretch applications: Atomicwork's Lead PD, SigNoz, Hevo Data, HeyMarvin, and the Zenara Health/xponentiate pair. All the AI design openings in India come from **US-incorporated startups with large Bengaluru teams**, not from the frontier labs. Anthropic, OpenAI, Cursor, Databricks, Glean and Harvey all run India offices or teams, but on 30 Sep 2026 every one of their India openings was in engineering or go-to-market. Their designers are hired in SF, NY, London or Toronto. Funding points to where the next roles will appear. **At least 13 AI companies with US HQ or incorporation and India teams raised money between Jan 2025 and Sep 2026.** They include Emergent ($70M Series B, Jan 2026; 70 of 75 staff in Bengaluru), Smallest.ai ($13M Series A, Jul 2026) and Gushwork ($9M seed, Feb 2026; about 70 staff in Bengaluru). Most of them had no design role visible on a public job board. The best strategy combines both signals: apply now where there is a live posting, and contact the design or founding team directly at the recently funded companies. Coverage has gaps. Workable, YC Work at a Startup, Dribbble and several JavaScript-rendered career pages could not be read. Several HQ cities come from board context rather than primary sources.

## Conclusion

For a mid-level designer in India, the useful unit of search is the **US-incorporated, Bengaluru-built AI startup at Series A or B**, not the US AI brand. These companies incorporate in Delaware or California to raise money and sell to US customers, but they build the product in India. That is why Ema, AiPrise and Atomicwork have design roles at the 3–4-year level while Anthropic, OpenAI and Cursor, despite new India offices, hire only go-to-market and engineering staff there. The gap between funding and posted roles is also an opening. Companies like Emergent and Gushwork have raised money and staffed up in Bengaluru, yet show no product-design posting. Firms at that stage often hire their first or second designer through referrals before a job description is ever written. A portfolio that shows AI-specific work (agent workflows, trust and error states, prompt and conversation UX, evaluation dashboards) will therefore get further through cold outreach than through ATS applications.

Looking ahead, the labs' India offices are the thing to watch in 2027. Glean's India product managers and Databricks' large R&D hub are the leading indicators that product design will follow engineering. When it does, the first India design roles at US AI labs will likely be senior. That makes 12–18 months at an AI-native startup today the most direct route into them later.
