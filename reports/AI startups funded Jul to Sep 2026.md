# Twenty-eight freshly funded AI startups want designers

The research notes log **273 AI companies worldwide that announced funding between 1 Jul and 30 Sep 2026**, worth roughly **$51B** in total. On 1 Oct 2026, **28 of them had a confirmed open Product Designer, UX or UI role**. Most of those 28 are US application-layer and vertical-AI companies at Series C or later: Harvey, Databricks, Rillet, Owner.com, Numeral, Profound, Factory, CodeRabbit, Fireworks, Horizon3.ai, Socure, Motive, Simile, Higharc and Candid Health. Earlier-stage US openings are at Meshy, Higgsfield, Comp AI and Ema (Series A–B), and at Clarion Health, Ande, Ando and Monogram (seed), where Clarion's role is a founding product designer. Only five non-US companies are hiring: Lovable (Stockholm), Helsing (Munich, Berlin, London or Paris), Sarvam and Emergent (both Bengaluru) and Onix (Montréal, in person). Two US companies are also hiring in India: Ema has a Product Designer role in Bengaluru, and Motive has a design-manager role in Bangalore. Round size does not predict design hiring. The biggest cheques went to compute, chips and frontier labs (SSI $5B, Databricks $5B, Crusoe $3B, Cognition $2B, Fluidstack $1.5B), and apart from Databricks those companies list no product-design roles at all. Treat 28 as a floor, not a census. **143 of the 273 companies (52%) were not checked** or could not be read, because no public Ashby, Greenhouse or Lever board was found. Founders for the US Series C+ companies also come from researcher background knowledge and are marked unverified throughout.

## Application-layer AI hires designers; compute and chips do not

The hit rate varies sharply by segment. Among the **43 US Series C+ and mega-round companies, 15 have a product-design opening**. The rate is 4 of 46 for US Series A–B, 4 of 34 for US seed, 2 of 73 for Europe, UK and Israel, 2 of 36 for India and APAC, and 1 of 41 for Canada, Latin America, the Middle East and Africa. The low non-US numbers mostly reflect coverage, not demand: 54 of the 73 European and Israeli rows and 31 of the 36 India/APAC rows were never checked. Where boards *were* checked, the dividing line is the product surface. Companies whose product is an interface that customers use every day are the ones hiring designers: legal AI (Harvey), ERP (Rillet), tax (Numeral), restaurant tools (Owner.com), AI-search visibility (Profound), code review (CodeRabbit) and app builders (Lovable, Emergent). Compute and silicon companies with confirmed boards and no product-design roles include **Crusoe** (349 jobs), **Fluidstack** (242), **Etched** (109), **SambaNova**, **Eliyan**, **Velaura AI** and **Gimlet Labs**. Their "design" postings are data-center, RTL or chip-layout work ([Crunchbase](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/biggest-funding-rounds-crusoe-fluidstack-multibillion-dollar-ai-infrastructure/)). Two well-known AI product companies also had no design roles on their boards: **Cognition** (103 jobs) and **Mistral AI** (210 jobs). That suggests their design teams are either small or recruited outside the ATS.

The money in the quarter went heavily to infrastructure and was raised at sharply rising valuations. Etched raised a $300M Series C at a **$10B pre-money valuation** in July, then $700M at **$21B** in August ([Crunchbase](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/biggest-funding-rounds-defense-tech-ai-infrastructure-castelion/)). Cognition's $2B round valued it at **$48B** ([Crunchbase](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/biggest-funding-rounds-boring-co-cognition-massive-week/)), up from a reported $26B in May ([Crunchbase](https://news.crunchbase.com/ai/biggest-funding-rounds-ai-anthropic-65b-dominates/)). Stage labels have also stopped tracking maturity. Glow's $180M **Series A** valued it at $1.2B ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/22/3331154/0/en/glow-emerges-from-stealth-with-180-million-to-reinvent-endpoint-security-in-the-ai-era.html)), River AI's ambiguous seed/Series A was **$1.1B** ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/11/general-catalyst-leads-1-1b-round-into-2-month-old-river-ai/)), and Walden Robotics' $300M was labelled a seed ([AlleyWatch](https://www.alleywatch.com/2026/08/global-startup-funding-top-largest-july-2026-vc/)). For a job seeker, this means a company's round letter says little about team size. Even a "Series A" company may already have a design team, or may not have hired its first designer yet.

By count, the dominant theme is **securing and operating AI agents**. Israel's quarter was led by Onyx Security ($113M, [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/israeli-cyber-startup-raises-113m-to-secure-and-control-autonomous-ai-agents/)), Zenity ($125M, [Zenity](https://zenity.io/company-overview/newsroom/company-news/zenity-raises-125-million-to-secure-the-era-of-1-billion-ai-agents)) and Alice ($140M, [Alice](https://alice.io/blog/alice-raises-140m)). In the US, Neo, Glow, AegisAI, HiddenLayer, MIND, Baselayer, Sapiom, Natural and Comp AI all sell some version of control, identity or payments for agents. These are mostly engineering-heavy security products, and only Comp AI shows a product-design role ([Rippling](https://ats.rippling.com/comp-ai/jobs/4d50ab31-cb50-462c-b0fc-a5b88239dc71)). ## Coverage gaps and what "not checked" means

**Sources used.** The US late-stage sweep read every Crunchbase News "Week's 10 Biggest Funding Rounds" column from the week ending 2 Jul to the week ending 25 Sep in full, plus company releases. The US Series A–B sweep combined those lists with TechStartups daily roundups (about 20 days), TechCrunch and wire releases. The seed sweep read every TechCrunch post matching "seed" in the window plus AlleyWatch's NYC daily reports. Europe drew on Tech.eu, Vestbee monthly lists, Sifted headlines and Calcalist for Israel. India and APAC used Inc42, Entrackr, YourStory, TNGlobal, Startup Daily and TechCrunch. Canada, Latin America, the Middle East and Africa used RSS from BetaKit, LatamList, TechCabal, Disrupt Africa and Wamda, plus Arab News startup wraps. Design hiring was checked on 1 Oct 2026 against the public Ashby, Greenhouse, Lever and Rippling APIs and, for the smaller regions, company careers pages. A board was counted only when its job descriptions or locations matched the company. Boards that turned out to belong to unrelated companies were discarded, for example Ashby "polar", "atomic", "sol" and "zero", and Greenhouse "air" and "axle".

**Blocked or paywalled.** Bloomberg, The Information, Axios Pro Rata, PitchBook, Sifted, DealStreetAsia, China Money Network, Techloy and harisq.com were paywalled and not used beyond headlines. EU-Startups round-ups, WOWTALE's Korean weekly and scouts.yutori.com returned HTTP 403. Disrupt Africa article pages were blocked by a firewall, so only feed titles were used, and Business Insider was refused by the search crawler. MAGNiTT, Contxto, LAVCA, Zawya and YC's Work at a Startup were not checked. Workable, Comeet, Teamtailor, Personio and the Japanese, Korean and Chinese ATS platforms were not queried.

**Weeks and areas with gaps.** The Series A–B sweep did not use TechStartups roundups for **1–7 Jul, 28 Jul–3 Aug, 25 Aug–2 Sep and 29–30 Sep**, so sub-$50M A rounds from those days are likely missing. The late-stage sweep could miss $100–150M rounds that did not make Crunchbase's top 10 in a busy week, as well as rounds from **26–30 Sep** (EliseAI and SiMa.ai were found separately). About 20 AlleyWatch daily reports did not resolve. US seed coverage outside New York relies on TechCrunch alone, with no systematic coverage of Boston, Los Angeles, Seattle or Austin. Vestbee reports European deals by month, so most mid-size European rows have only a month as the date. Calcalist's Israeli list was truncated, which leaves July Israeli rounds under-covered. Korea, Southeast Asia, Mexico and Colombia are thin.

**What "not checked" means.** It means no public Ashby, Greenhouse or Lever board was found under the slugs tried, or the careers page was JavaScript-rendered and could not be read. It says nothing about whether the company is hiring. That applies to **141 rows**, plus 2 "undetermined" rows (ChatFeatured, which lists two unread roles, and Axya). Among them are well-funded, product-heavy companies such as Instinct, Paper (itself a design tool), PixVerse, Fish Audio, Flam, Cyera, Zenity, Lyzr and HappyRobot's wider team. These are worth checking by hand or approaching directly.

**Unverified fields and excluded items.** All US Series C+ founders and most of their websites are [U]. Several dates are only a Crunchbase week or a month. Groq's $3.5B valuation conflicts with past reports and was not reconciled. Rows kept with flags include Strattum (closed in June, covered on 4 Aug), Velocity (date "07/09/2026" is ambiguous), Ditto (one snippet gives 28 Sep), Nscale (debt) and borderline-AI companies (Candid Health, Ridgeline, Island, Spectro Cloud, Skello, Claryx, Cymphony, Dawraty, Viabot). The notes checked and excluded these: 8090 ($135M Series A, 26 Jun), Prosper AI, Architect Labs, Radical Numerics and Mirendil (all June), and Mind Robotics and Rhoda AI (March). ElevenLabs' $300M at $22B on 30 Sep was an employee tender, not a primary round ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/30/ai-voice-startup-elevenlabs-doubles-valuation-to-22b/)), so it is excluded. Mistral is in the Europe table, but other non-US late-stage names mentioned in passing (Moonshot, Kling) were not profiled. Items backed only by unfetched search snippets were left out, including Inevitable AI Group, Abwab.ai, Sinai.ai, Kudwa and nine small September European AI deals from Tech.eu recaps that had no product details. Borderline APAC items were also left out: Thinking Machines (closer to an acquisition), Dnotitia and HyperAccel (probably before the window).

## Conclusion

For a product designer, the quarter's funding data is most useful as a filter, not a ranking. The capital went to compute, but design jobs follow the interface. That makes the best targets the vertical and application-layer AI companies that just raised Series B to D rounds. Their products are used every day by lawyers, accountants, restaurateurs and developers, and they have the money to build design teams. Harvey, Rillet, Numeral, Owner.com, Profound, Factory, CodeRabbit and Lovable fit that profile, and every one is hiring now. The seed list is the place to find founding-designer roles, where an early hire shapes the product, and Clarion Health shows that such roles exist even at $10M.

The larger opportunity is in the half of the list nobody checked. More than 140 companies raised money this quarter and have no readable job board. Many of them, especially the consumer and creative AI startups in Europe, India and Asia, will hire their first or second designer through referrals before posting a role. A short, AI-specific portfolio (agent workflows, trust and error states, evaluation dashboards) sent directly to founders at these companies is likely to reach more live openings than the 28 visible ones. The companies to start with have strong consumer surfaces and fresh money: Instinct, Paper, PixVerse, Flam, Fish Audio, Wrtn and Heidi.
