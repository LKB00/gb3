    Why we built it this way

    Most AI trading tools ask you to trust a black box. We ask you to read the rules.

    SEBI’s 2025 framework for retail algorithmic trading treats strategies whose logic is hidden from the user as a separate, more regulated category. We chose the other path: every strategy is a document you can read, edit and approve.

    Rules

    1. Hold the ten Nifty 100 stocks with the strongest 6-month price momentum, equal weight.
    2. Rebalance on the first trading day of each month.
    3. Sell any holding that falls 8% below its entry price.
    4. Never deploy more than ₹10,000 in total.

    Why these stocks

    Each holding is in the top decile of 6-month momentum and trades at least ₹5 crore a day, so orders of this size fill without moving the price.

    Data used

    NSE end-of-day prices, adjusted for corporate actions, from [data vendor]. Brokerage, STT and exchange charges applied per trade.

    What could go wrong

    Momentum strategies lag in sharp reversals. In the simulated period the worst month was −11.4%. Costs and slippage reduce results further. Simulated results do not predict future performance.

    What the AI does

    Drafts rules from your prompt

    It reads your prompt and drafts a rule set from [data sources]. It does not place discretionary trades and does not learn from your account in real time.

    What it can get wrong

    Misread you, or overfit the past

    It can misread an ambiguous prompt or draft rules that fit history too neatly. That is why you see every rule and a labelled test before anything runs.

    What we will not claim

    No accuracy. No guarantees.

    No accuracy percentages, no guaranteed returns, no “beats the market”. Backtests are simulated and do not predict future results.

    We started FinStocks because the tools that trade for you never showed you why. A strategy you cannot read is a strategy you cannot trust.
