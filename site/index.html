    How our AI works, and its limits

    AI drafts the rules. The rules do the trading. You decide.

    What the AI does
    It reads your prompt and drafts a rule set from [data sources]. It does not place discretionary trades and it does not learn from your account in real time.
    What it can get wrong
    It can misread an ambiguous prompt or draft rules that fit the past too neatly. That is why you see every rule and a labelled test before anything runs.
    What we will not claim
    No accuracy percentages, no guaranteed returns, no “beats the market”. Backtests are simulated and do not predict future results.

    Who holds my money?
    You do. Funds and shares stay in your own trading and demat account with your broker. FinStocks AI only sends order instructions you have authorised, and never has access to withdraw.
    Which brokers can I connect?
    [Broker list]. Connection uses the broker’s own login. We never see your broker password.

    Nothing runs until you approve it. Once live, your loss limit pauses everything automatically, and you can pause or close positions yourself in your broker app at any time. Losses are possible and are yours; no strategy is guaranteed.

    Yes. One tap pauses Autopilot. Pausing sends no new orders; existing holdings stay in your account until you decide what to do with them.
