No black box

Every strategy is a document, not a secret.

SEBI’s 2025 rules treat strategies whose logic is hidden from you as a separate, more regulated category. We chose the other path.

What the AI does
Drafts rules from your prompt using [data sources]. It never improvises a trade.
What it can get wrong
It can misread a vague prompt or fit the past too neatly. That’s why you see every rule and a labelled test first.
What we won’t claim
No accuracy percentages, no guaranteed returns, no “beats the market”.

Rules

  1. Hold the ten Nifty 100 stocks with the strongest 6-month price momentum, equal weight.
  2. Rebalance on the first trading day of each month.
  3. Sell any holding that falls 8% below its entry price.
  4. Never deploy more than ₹10,000 in total.

Why these stocks

Each holding is in the top decile of 6-month momentum and trades at least ₹5 crore a day, so orders of this size fill without moving the price.

Data used

NSE end-of-day prices, adjusted for corporate actions, from [data vendor]. Costs applied per trade.

What could go wrong

Momentum strategies lag in sharp reversals. In the simulated period the worst month was −11.4%. Simulated results do not predict future performance.
